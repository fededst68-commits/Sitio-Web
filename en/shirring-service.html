Shirring and ready-to-use treatment for your own fibrous casing

If you already buy fibrous casing, we shirr it into sticks and apply the RTU (ready-to-use) treatment in our plant. It comes back in boxes, ready for the stuffer: less handling and less line time.

How it works

  • You send us the fibrous casing on reels or in the format you receive it.
  • We shirr it into sticks of the length your stuffer needs and apply the RTU treatment.
  • We return it in boxes, ready to use without soaking.

What you gain

No casing supplier yet? See our Fibrø® fibrous casing, which already comes shirred and RTU-treated.

  • Less handling per piece and fewer stops on the line.
  • No soaking buckets and no waiting time.
  • Sticks compatible with automatic stuffing and clipping equipment.

Frequently asked questions

Do you shirr casing of any brand?

Yes. We work with the fibrous casing you already use, whatever its origin.

What stick length can you make?

Whatever your stuffer needs. Tell us the equipment and the calibre and we adjust it.

How long does it take?

It depends on the volume. We confirm the lead time when quoting.

Tell us what you make and we will quote.

Tell us the product, the calibre or net you use today and the approximate volume. We reply with a proposal and, if needed, a sample.
