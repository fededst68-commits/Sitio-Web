Netting for coppa

20-square elastic net on a 50 metre roll, in three widths depending on the diameter of the piece. With double elastic for heavy or long-cured coppa.

Which model to choose

Choose by the diameter of the coppa: Coppa No. 3 for thin pieces, No. 4 for medium and No. 5 for thick. Double elastic (two threads together in the weft) adds hold.

Frequently asked questions

Roll or tube?

We deliver on 50 metre rolls. If you load tubes, tell us the tube diameter and we prepare the net for that load.

Which colour?

White, red, natural or the colour you ask for.

Tell us what you make and we will quote.

Tell us the product, the calibre or net you use today and the approximate volume. We reply with a proposal and, if needed, a sample.
