What it is and what it is for

Stockinette is a continuous knitted tube. It is cut to the length of the piece and wraps it completely: it holds, protects the surface and lets smoke and heat reach the product evenly.

We knit it in our plant, in the same area where we make Enets® nets, and deliver it on rolls.

Sizes and yarns

Standard width is 20 cm; other widths are made to the customer's request. Tell us the type of piece, its diameter and your process (smoking or curing) and we will recommend the size and yarn.

Frequently asked questions

How is stockinette different from a net?

A net is a mesh of squares that marks and compresses the piece; stockinette is a closed knit that wraps it completely without marking it.

What width is it?

Standard width is 20 cm. Other widths are made to order.

How is it delivered?

On rolls, to be cut to the length each piece needs.

Tell us what you make and we will quote.

Tell us the product, the calibre or net you use today and the approximate volume. We reply with a proposal and, if needed, a sample.
