Fibrø®

Fibrous casing for cooked, cured and dry products.

A strong, uniform casing for salami, coppa, ham and mortadella. What sets it apart, step by step.

Regenerated cellulosefibre paper, strong and permeable

  1. Fibre-reinforced regenerated cellulose

    A long-fibre paper impregnated with regenerated cellulose. That is why it withstands stuffing, keeps its calibre through the whole run and lets smoke and steam through. Depending on the inner treatment, it clings to the product or peels easily.

  2. Shirred in our plant

    Metres of casing shirred into one stick in our plant, compatible with automatic stuffing and clipping equipment. Less handling per piece and, on request, impregnated with aromas and flavours.

  3. Ready to use

    The RTU treatment is applied during shirring: the stick comes out ready to use, with no soaking. It leaves the box and goes straight into the stuffer, with no buckets and no waiting time on the line.

  4. Calibres Ø45 to Ø195

    From straight salami to mortadella. Keep scrolling to run through the calibres and see which product each one is for.

    Straight salami Ø45 to 58 Milano salami Ø80 to 107 Coppa and pork belly Ø107 to 165 Ham and mortadella Ø165 to 195

Nets that go with the product from curing to the point of sale.

Even hold during curing and cooking, good ventilation and a neat final presentation. Choose yarn, look, colour and compression.

Net typeelastic

  1. Elastic or non-elastic

    Elastic for coppa, loin, pork belly, bresaola and rolled roasts. Non-elastic, diamond or hexagonal mesh, for ham, mortadella and Milano salami. Keep scrolling to see both.

  2. Custom compression

    Standard models or the exact compression your product needs, to follow shrinkage during curing. With double elastic, two threads together in the weft for extra hold: keep scrolling to see it.

  3. Yarn and colour

    Polyester, cotton, polyethylene or compostable material. White or colours on request, so the net also sells on the shelf.

  4. Squares and rolls

    14, 20, 44 or 60 squares, on 50 and 100 metre rolls. Adjustable square size on non-elastic nets.

    14 squares: rolled roasts 20: coppa, loin, pork belly 44: jamoneta 60: ham, 100 m

  5. Kosher

    Elastic and non-elastic nets for kosher-certified cuts, such as huachalomo and roast beef, with a maximum stretch of 24 to 44 cm depending on the model.
