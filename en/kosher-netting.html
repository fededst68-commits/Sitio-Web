Elastic or non-elastic

The elastic version follows the shrinkage of the piece during the process; the wide-square non-elastic version contains without compressing. Both are knitted in our plant with the yarn and colour you ask for.

Frequently asked questions

Which model do I use for huachalomo?

It depends on the diameter of the piece: from Kosher standard (up to 24 cm stretched) to Kosher 44 (up to 44 cm). We send samples to try on your line.

Do they come on rolls?

Yes, on 50 metre rolls like the rest of the Enets® elastic nets.

Tell us what you make and we will quote.

Tell us the product, the calibre or net you use today and the approximate volume. We reply with a proposal and, if needed, a sample.
