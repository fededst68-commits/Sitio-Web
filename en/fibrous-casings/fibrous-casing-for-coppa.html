How to choose the calibre

The calibre is the diameter of the casing in millimetres. For coppa it goes from Ø107 (small pieces) to Ø165 (large pieces). If you hesitate between two, we send samples of both.

The complete coppa

After the casing, 20-square elastic net (Coppa No. 3, 4 or 5) and cellophane in 45×50, 50×60 or 70×90 cm.

Frequently asked questions

Meat cling or easy peel?

For coppa sold without the casing, easy peel. Tell us how you sell it and we choose the inner treatment.

Tell us what you make and we will quote.

Tell us the product, the calibre or net you use today and the approximate volume. We reply with a proposal and, if needed, a sample.
