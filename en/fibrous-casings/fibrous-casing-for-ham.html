Fibrous casing for ham

The large calibres of the line, Ø165 to Ø195, for cooked ham and mortadella. Shirred into sticks with RTU treatment, ready for the stuffer.

Large calibres, same treatment

Large-calibre casing keeps an even diameter through the whole run and lets cooking steam through. With the RTU treatment it is stuffed without soaking, like the rest of the Fibrø® line.

The complete ham

60-square non-elastic net (Ham 40 or Ham 60) and 70×90 cm cellophane or mesh bag.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for mortadella?

Yes, the same Ø165 to Ø195 calibres are used for mortadella, with a made-to-measure non-elastic net.
